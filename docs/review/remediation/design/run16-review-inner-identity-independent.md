## Decision and required owning boundaries

The design requires no revision before root's bounded implementation decision.
Its scope follows the observed original producer failure, not a global
serialization refactor:

- Policy resolver owns selected and ALL source-provenance digests. The same
  parsed policy value, precedence and authorized-override semantics remain.
  Private resolver ownership must retain the selected mode; reducer validation
  must recompute using that authority and an independently expected semantic
  context, not trust a caller digest or portable-looking property.
- Initial, slice and expanded snapshot creation must pass that same original
  graph-owned context before generating facts and candidate evidence. Both
  governor branches and report construction must reject cross-mode resolver
  ownership. Context/scope/limit authority remains its separate original owner.
- Generated facts and candidate manifests keep original semantic schemas,
  scanning, rules, limits and IDs. Their existing evidence encoding marker
  selects portable content bytes; candidate source.digest comes from the actual
  generated source bytes. The original proof establishes equal candidate IDs
  for its admitted case, not universal locale independence of every identity.
- The manifest WeakMap certificate must retain revision identity, policy identity
  and the selected byte-codec/context. Its public verification path and the
  separate persisted simplificationState reader both remain covered. The latter
  checks actual content digest, parsed schema, revision and registry; it is not
  replaced by an in-memory certificate assertion.
- Original review-bundle-bounds parses actual inner evidence and compares
  reviewEvidenceJson under that evidence's own encoding to its exact content.
  Therefore a new generated tag is required output, not cosmetic metadata.
  Existing independently admitted external evidence keeps its original bytes,
  digest and tag/absence. Identical bytes admitted by both codecs do not become
  cryptographically distinct merely because a marker is absent.
- Omitted original resolver/digest/fact/manifest/helper APIs remain historical.
  Repository-audit and other unselected producers must not silently adopt the
  new mode. No cache/source/report outer marker, compiler freshness or current
  locale authorizes these inner algorithms.

The still-unpublished review-semantics.utf16-v1 selector may own this bounded
extension only as part of the first atomic coupled semantic cutover. Publishing
that selector with one policy/fact algorithm and changing it later under the
same persisted authority is explicitly not approved. Bundle.v2, governor.v2 and
report.v3 pairing, immutable compiler graph recovery and Summary expectations
remain the separately approved semantic package's responsibilities.
