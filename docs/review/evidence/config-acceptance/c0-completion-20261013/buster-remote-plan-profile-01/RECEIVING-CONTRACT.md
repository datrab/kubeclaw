- **recordLimits:** all three are positive safe integers. `maximumRecordBytes` applies the **whole PreparedPlanJobRecord payload**, including input/job/source/resultOrigin/status, not just the plan or request. `maximumBytes` applies the complete canonical store state with record headers, identities, digests, timestamps, sequences, tombstones/retirements where present and array/schema delimiters. Do not require maxRecords*maxRecordBytes<=maxBytes: these are independent ceilings, not guaranteed simultaneous allocation. The published accounting shim explicitly does not validate a real source/job.
- **maximumResultBytes:** actual `#complete` canonicalizes the full RemotePlanResultV1 wrapper including plan/run/job/result identity, all result entries and artifact metadata before comparing bytes.
